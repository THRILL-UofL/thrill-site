Who We Are

Built by engineers
obsessed with rides.

THRILL is a student engineering organization at the University of Louisville's J.B. Speed School of Engineering. We exist to bridge the gap between academic engineering and the themed entertainment industry.

Our members compete in national ride engineering competitions, connect with industry professionals, and produce the educational events and content that make the amusement industry accessible to the next generation of engineers.

Ride Engineering Competition

Block Party.
Hersheypark. 2027.

THRILL's competition team designs, builds, and operates a fully functional scale roller coaster, judged on engineering, safety, theming, and real ride performance. This year's challenge: two trains running simultaneously with block-zone safety logic.

Competition
April 10, 2027 at Hersheypark, PA
Challenge
Block Party: two trains operating simultaneously with PLCs, sensors, and block-zone safety logic
Riders
Jolly Rancher chews
Track
Gravity Group-style vertical laminate wood, laser-cut and puzzle-fitted
Envelope
1000mm wide × 900mm tall × 750mm deep
Scoring
1,000 pts across Systems Report, Acceptance Tests, Functional Ride, Theming, and Team Outreach
Team Size
Around 10 core members, up to 28 registered

Roles on the Team

Train Team

Car bodies, bogie geometry, wheel systems, restraints, anti-rollback, and brake fins

Supports Team

Track structure, vertical laminate rail, support columns, FEA, and fatigue analysis

Controls Team

PLC programming, block zone logic, sensors, and safety-related control systems

Theming Team

Ride theme, visual design, marketability scoring, and outreach materials

Partner With Us

Support the next
generation of ride engineers.

THRILL connects your brand with motivated engineering students at the University of Louisville, students who will go on to design, build, and operate the rides your customers love.

Why Partner With THRILL

What your support makes possible.

01

Talent Pipeline

Get your brand in front of UofL Speed School students before they graduate. THRILL members are actively pursuing careers in themed entertainment, mechanical, civil, and controls engineering.

02

Brand Visibility

Your logo on our website, event materials, and Industry Inversion newsletter, seen by students, faculty, and industry professionals across the region.

03

Competition Support

Help fund our Ride Engineering Competition team competing at Hersheypark in April 2027. Sponsors are recognized on the ride itself and in our engineering report.

04

Community Impact

Support events like CoasterVille that bring engineering education to the broader UofL campus and help grow the next wave of themed entertainment professionals.
