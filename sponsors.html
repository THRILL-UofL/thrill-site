Partner With Us

Support the next
generation of ride engineers.

THRILL connects your brand with motivated engineering students at the University of Louisville, students who will go on to design, build, and operate the rides your customers love.

Why Partner With THRILL

What your support makes possible.

01

Talent Pipeline

Get your brand in front of UofL Speed School students before they graduate. THRILL members are actively pursuing careers in themed entertainment, mechanical, civil, and controls engineering.

02

Brand Visibility

Your logo on our website, event materials, and Industry Inversion newsletter, seen by students, faculty, and industry professionals across the region.

03

Competition Support

Help fund our Ride Engineering Competition team competing at Hersheypark in April 2027. Sponsors are recognized on the ride itself and in our engineering report.

04

Community Impact

Support events like CoasterVille that bring engineering education to the broader UofL campus and help grow the next wave of themed entertainment professionals.
