Who We Are

Built by engineers
obsessed with rides.

THRILL is a student engineering organization at the University of Louisville's J.B. Speed School of Engineering. We exist to bridge the gap between academic engineering and the themed entertainment industry.

Our members compete in national ride engineering competitions, connect with industry professionals, and produce the educational events and content that make the amusement industry accessible to the next generation of engineers.
