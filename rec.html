Ride Engineering Competition

Block Party.
Hersheypark. 2027.

THRILL's competition team designs, builds, and operates a fully functional scale roller coaster, judged on engineering, safety, theming, and real ride performance. This year's challenge: two trains running simultaneously with block-zone safety logic.

Competition
April 10, 2027 at Hersheypark, PA
Challenge
Block Party: two trains operating simultaneously with PLCs, sensors, and block-zone safety logic
Riders
Jolly Rancher chews
Track
Gravity Group-style vertical laminate wood, laser-cut and puzzle-fitted
Envelope
1000mm wide × 900mm tall × 750mm deep
Scoring
1,000 pts across Systems Report, Acceptance Tests, Functional Ride, Theming, and Team Outreach
Team Size
Around 10 core members, up to 28 registered

Roles on the Team

Train Team

Car bodies, bogie geometry, wheel systems, restraints, anti-rollback, and brake fins

Supports Team

Track structure, vertical laminate rail, support columns, FEA, and fatigue analysis

Controls Team

PLC programming, block zone logic, sensors, and safety-related control systems

Theming Team

Ride theme, visual design, marketability scoring, and outreach materials
