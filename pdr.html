The Review

What we will present

Ride Concept
Our layout, theme, and train design for this year's Block Party challenge: two trains running at the same time with block-zone safety controls.
Risk Assessment
The hazards we have found so far, scored by severity and probability, with ASTM F3598 as our guide.
Sub-team Plans
Where Train, Track and Supports, Controls, and Theming stand, and what each team builds next.
Your Feedback
Questions, concerns, and anything you would do differently. This is the part we need most.

Reviewers

Who we hope to hear from

Mechanical Engineers

Track structure, supports, trains, wheel assemblies, and brakes

Electrical and Controls Engineers

PLCs, sensors, and block zone logic for two trains on one circuit

Ride Safety, Operations, and Maintenance

Risk assessment, inspections, and what actually happens on a ride every day

Designers and Creatives

Theming, storytelling, and the guest experience

The REC rulebook strongly recommends outside reviewers from mechanical and electrical or controls engineering, so your time makes a real difference for our team.
